The day at a glance

Start from the south of Skye, head north through Portree, then follow the Trotternish coast and return. The two headline landscapes are the Old Man of Storr and the Quiraing; keep the smaller stops flexible.

Our simple rule

Skye is better when the plan can bend. Check visibility before committing to the longer walks, and enjoy a viewpoint stop without turning every stop into a hike.

Protect the best bits: prioritise Storr and the Quiraing when visibility is good. Shorten the day by skipping Fairy Glen or a Portree stop.

Pick the pace that suits you

Viewpoints + relaxed walking

  • Keep Storr to the lower section or viewpoint if the full walk feels too much.
  • Enjoy Lealt Falls and Kilt Rock as short stops.
  • Take in the Quiraing from the roadside viewpoint.
  • Choose either Fairy Glen or extra time in Portree.

Add a longer walk

  • Give Storr a dedicated window and check the route and conditions first.
  • At the Quiraing, walk only as far as feels comfortable, then turn back.
  • Drop optional stops before rushing the return drive.
  • Carry waterproof layers and shoes with grip.
For mixed-energy groups: agree on a meeting point and return time before anyone starts a longer walk. No one needs to complete the same route for everyone to enjoy the stop.

Before you set off

  • Check the local forecast and visibility again on the morning.
  • Check current parking arrangements, road conditions and any closures.
  • Fuel up before heading into quieter parts of the island.
  • Download an offline map; mobile signal can vary.
  • Bring a waterproof layer even when the start looks bright.
Keep it safe: use designated parking and passing places correctly; don’t stop on the road for photos. Check current CAA rules, airspace, local signs and landowner guidance before flying a drone.

Opening times, parking, access rules, weather and road conditions can change. Recheck them for your travel date.
