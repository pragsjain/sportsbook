Series strategy

Use firsthand footage and honest experiences: Quiraing drone shots, Eilean Donan aerials, Harry Potter shop in Edinburgh → Glenfinnan train, driving roads, hairy coos, rainbows, Skye Bridge, Irn-Bru, kilts, Scottish wool, rose hips, Indian restaurants, Pitlochry and Caol/Kyleakin stays.

Reels build discovery through voiceover, personality and cinematic footage. Carousels deliver saveable maps and RGF. Each episode should work independently, with a small consistent “MostlyGhumo | Scotland Series” label.

Publish the hero reel and master route first; then follow the journey chronologically, interleaving experience reels. No separate duplicate road-trip trailer.

The existing day guides are pre-trip plans and may not match every stop. Actual experience wins. Day 5 was via A86/A9 and Pitlochry, not Loch Ness/Inverness.

Workflow and RGF

Workflow: Idea → Script → Approved → Media selected → Editing → Ready → Posted.

RGF format: TIME Place (⭐ priority | km from previous | Stop | Walk | Rest/food | ⏰ closing if relevant).

Each day carousel needs a clean 4:5 visual map, a condensed readable RGF, genuine photos and tips; link to the original interactive map for the full route. Add an exportable map image later.
