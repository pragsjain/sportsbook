Detailed plan

Change the start time and the flexible itinerary below shifts automatically. Booked or genuinely fixed-time events stay fixed; if a shifted item collides with one, follow the fixed event.

08:00–09:00 — Breakfast, get ready and pack at Caol accommodation in Caol.
09:15–09:25 — Check out and leave Caol. Drive directly toward Glenfinnan; do not add Fort William sightseeing this morning.
~09:55–10:05 — Arrive at Glenfinnan visitor/community parking. Parking can be very busy, so use only marked bays.
10:05–10:25 — Walk toward the viaduct viewpoint. The lower viaduct view is about a 15-minute flat walk; the elevated view adds roughly another 10-minute uphill climb with some steps. Parents can use the lower view while fitter members continue higher.
10:25 onward — Be in position and wait. The train is NOT scheduled for 10:25: this is your safe ready-at-viewpoint time.
~10:45–11:00 — ⭐ Jacobite / “Harry Potter train” crossing. The 2026 morning Jacobite leaves Fort William at 10:15; Glenfinnan crossing time is approximate and can vary, so stay ready rather than relying on one exact minute.
Backup if missed: the next westbound Jacobite is the afternoon service, leaving Fort William at 12:50 and expected over Glenfinnan roughly 13:20–13:30. The later crossing is an option, but waiting for it would delay the rest of the Skye route by about 2½ hours. There is also an eastbound return crossing around 15:00–15:20. Recommendation: protect the morning crossing and use the 13:20–13:30 train only as Plan B.
~11:10–11:20 — Leave Glenfinnan and drive back east toward Spean Bridge.
~12:00 — Commando Memorial — about 20 minutes. Very little walking.
~13:15 — Loch Garry viewpoint — 15–20 minute photo stop.
~14:45–15:45 — Eilean Donan Castle. Prioritise the exterior/causeway viewpoint; go inside only if the day is comfortably on time.
~16:00 — Continue toward Kyle of Lochalsh and cross the Skye Bridge.
~16:15–16:30 onward — Check in to Kyleakin accommodation, Kyleakin. Check-in is available after 16:00. Castle Moil is optional only if everyone still has energy.
18:30–19:00 — Dinner in/around Kyleakin; keep the evening relaxed.

👟 Walking plan — parent-friendly

Overall: moderate but very flexible. The easy version is roughly 1.5–3 km total walking spread through the whole day.

Glenfinnan: choose the lower viaduct view for your parents — it is about a 15-minute flat walk from the visitor parking. The upper viewpoint adds roughly 10 minutes uphill with steps and can be slippery, so only the fitter members need to do it.

Commando Memorial / Loch Garry: short photo stops with very little walking.

Eilean Donan: parents can enjoy the classic exterior/causeway views without touring every part of the castle. If they are comfortable with stairs, go inside; otherwise keep this to a relaxed viewpoint stop.

Kyleakin: Castle Moil is optional. Do not add it just because it is nearby if everyone is already tired.

Flexible split: younger/fitter travellers can take the upper Glenfinnan path while parents stay at the lower viewpoint and café/visitor area.
