Tuesday 22 September — Skye to Edinburgh via Pitlochry

Kyleakin → A86 / A9 → Pitlochry → Edinburgh → train south

Day planRoute mapStops & notes
🚗 Highlands to Edinburgh🌿 Scenic return

Detailed plan

Morning — Have breakfast, pack, and leave Kyleakin with enough time for a relaxed drive east and south.
On the road — Follow the main route through the Highlands, connecting to the A86 and then the A9 south. Keep the schedule flexible for road conditions and short comfort stops.
Pitlochry — Pause in town for lunch, a leg stretch and a walk along the River Tummel. If time allows, browse the high street or visit the dam and fish ladder area.
Afternoon — Continue south to Edinburgh. Return the car, then head to Waverley for the evening train.
19:20 — Scheduled direct train from Edinburgh to London King’s Cross; leave a generous buffer for the car return and station transfer.

Tuesday route map

Stops and practical notes

Pitlochry

A useful midpoint to break up the drive. The town centre, riverside paths and dam area make for an easy stop without committing to a long hike.

Keep the return day simple

Choose one relaxed stop in Pitlochry, keep an eye on the clock, and allow extra time for traffic on the A9 and the journey into Edinburgh.
