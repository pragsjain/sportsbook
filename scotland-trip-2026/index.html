Day 1 — Edinburgh arrival

Waverley Station taxi tip

After arriving at Edinburgh Waverley, we found the official black-cab / central taxi rank right outside the station. It was quicker and easier than waiting for an Uber, especially with luggage. For our stay near the Royal Mile, taking the station taxi straight to the apartment was the smoothest option.
