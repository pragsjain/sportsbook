Sunday 20 September — Glenfinnan to Skye

Caol/Fort William → Glenfinnan → Spean Bridge → Loch Garry → Eilean Donan → Kyleakin

🚗 ~129 miles~4h driving + sightseeing🏨 Clarion Cottage, Kyleakin

Detailed plan

08:00 — Breakfast and leave Caol early.
08:30–10:45 — Glenfinnan Viaduct. Park early; if you want the Jacobite crossing, confirm the 2026 timetable the day before.
11:30 — Return east toward Spean Bridge. Stop at Commando Memorial for ~20 minutes.
13:30–14:00 — Loch Garry viewpoint. Quick photo stop.
15:00–16:00 — Eilean Donan Castle. Exterior/viewpoint is enough if you want to protect check-in time.
16:15 — Cross Skye Bridge.
16:30 onward — Check in to Clarion Cottage, Kyleakin. Short Castle Moil walk if everyone still has energy.
18:30–19:00 — Dinner in Kyleakin. Taste of India is the easiest option.

Sunday map

Must-do: Glenfinnan + Eilean Donan. Quick stops: Commando Memorial + Loch Garry. Skip if late: Eilean Donan interior, not the viewpoint.

Weather plan

Coastal and changeable. Expect roughly 13–15°C, gusty spells and short showers. Keep rain jackets accessible rather than buried in luggage.
