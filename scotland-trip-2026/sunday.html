👟 Walking plan — parent-friendly

Overall: moderate but very flexible. The easy version is roughly 1.5–3 km total walking spread through the whole day.

Glenfinnan: choose the lower viaduct view for your parents — it is about a 15-minute flat walk from the visitor parking. The upper viewpoint adds roughly 10 minutes uphill with steps and can be slippery, so only the fitter members need to do it.

Commando Memorial / Loch Garry: short photo stops with very little walking.

Eilean Donan: parents can enjoy the classic exterior/causeway views without touring every part of the castle. If they are comfortable with stairs, go inside; otherwise keep this to a relaxed viewpoint stop.

Kyleakin: Castle Moil is optional. Do not add it just because it is nearby if everyone is already tired.

Flexible split: younger/fitter travellers can take the upper Glenfinnan path while parents stay at the lower viewpoint and café/visitor area.
