Must-do: get to Edinburgh safely with buffer. Worth one stop: Fort Augustus or Urquhart Castle. Skip if behind: Inverness sightseeing, Culloden, Pitlochry. Missing the 19:20 train would be much worse than missing a viewpoint.

👟 Walking plan — parent-friendly

Overall: keep Tuesday deliberately light — roughly 1–2 km easy walking at most. The priority is the long drive and the fixed 19:20 train.

Fort Augustus: best parent stop of the day. The canal/lock area is relatively flat and easy to enjoy with a short 20–30 minute wander.

Urquhart Castle: the site has steep ramps and some stairs. For an easier visit, use the visitor centre/viewpoint and do not descend through the full ruins. The site has wheelchairs and mobility scooters on a first-come basis if needed.

Inverness: keep lunch close to parking. No city walking loop.

Flexible rule: if anyone is tired, skip Urquhart ruins entirely and keep just Fort Augustus + lunch. Saving energy and protecting the Edinburgh arrival buffer matters more today.

Skip first if behind schedule: Urquhart interior, then Inverness sightseeing. Do not sacrifice the car-return/train buffer.

Weather plan

Transport-critical day. Western Highlands may be wet or foggy while Edinburgh is dry. Keep at least 60 minutes of road-weather margin and check Traffic Scotland before departure.
