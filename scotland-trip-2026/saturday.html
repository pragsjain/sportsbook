Saturday 19 September — Highlands to Fort William

Edinburgh → Stirling → Callander → Rannoch Moor → Glencoe → Glen Etive → Caol

🚗 ~155 miles~4h45 driving + stops🏨 15 By The Shore

Confirmed car

Keddy / Europcar — Volkswagen T-Roc or similar, automatic, 5 seats. Pickup 10:00 at 65–67 Salamander St, Leith. Mileage allowance 800 miles; fuel full-to-full.

Detailed plan

08:30–09:00 — Breakfast, pack and check out from Edinburgh apartment.
09:15 — Leave for Europcar Leith. Be at the desk before 10:00; voucher says pickup must be completed by 10:29.
10:00–10:30 — Car pickup, inspect exterior/interior, photograph damage, confirm fuel, mileage and Tuesday return instructions.
11:15–12:15 — Stirling Castle area. If you want to go inside, keep this to about an hour; otherwise viewpoint/exterior and continue.
13:00 — Callander lunch. If running late, skip Stirling interior and make Callander the first proper stop.
14:15 — Tyndrum fuel/coffee stop, then Bridge of Orchy and Rannoch Moor.
15:30–16:15 — Glencoe: Three Sisters viewpoint + Meeting of Three Waters / Loch Achtriochtan.
16:30–18:15 — Glen Etive out-and-back. Single-track road, go slowly; this is the most photogenic late-day section.
19:15–19:45 — Reach 15 By The Shore, Caol/Fort William. Check-in is from 16:00.
20:00 — Dinner in Fort William. Spice Tandoori is the obvious Indian option.

Saturday map

Must-do: Rannoch Moor + Three Sisters + Glencoe. Best optional: Glen Etive. Skip if late: Stirling Castle interior first, then Glen Etive if weather turns poor.

Weather plan

Western Highlands are typically wetter and windier than Edinburgh. Expect cool 12–15°C conditions, showers and rapid changes. Waterproof jacket and good shoes are essential.
