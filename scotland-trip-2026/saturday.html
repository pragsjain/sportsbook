⏱️ How long at each stop + how much walking

Rule for today: protect time for Rannoch Moor + Glencoe. Tyndrum is functional, Stirling is deliberately controlled, and Glen Etive expands or contracts depending on the clock. This avoids spending an hour at a minor stop and rushing the Highlands.

1. Stirling Castle area — 35–45 min | LOW–MEDIUM priority
Walking: roughly 500 m–1 km if using nearby city parking; uphill/cobbled approach. The castle itself is on a steep hill. Do: castle exterior, esplanade/view, quick photos. Interior: only if you deliberately choose it; a proper interior visit deserves much longer and will squeeze the Highlands. Current official notice says ordinary cars cannot use the castle car park; it is limited to coaches and accessible parking.
2. Callander + Desh Indian lunch — 60–75 min | REST / FOOD priority
Walking: about 200–500 m depending where you park. Keep this as your proper break. Desh Restaurant is at 101 Main Street and serves Indian food with vegetarian choices. Do not add a separate café lunch elsewhere.
3. Tyndrum — 10–15 min | LOW priority
Walking: under 200 m. Fuel, toilet, coffee if required, then leave. Do not spend sightseeing time here.
4. Rannoch Moor / Loch Tulla — 20–25 min total | MUST-DO
Walking: roughly 100–300 m total across roadside stops. Pick 1–2 safe viewpoints, step out, take in the moor, lochans and huge open landscape, photos/video, then continue. No hike needed.
5. Glencoe / Three Sisters — 45–60 min | ⭐ HIGHEST priority
Walking: roughly 300–700 m total if you keep to viewpoints. Spend the most sightseeing time here. First priority: Three Sisters viewpoint. Then: Meeting of Three Waters and/or Loch Achtriochtan if parking and weather work. Do not start a mountain trail today; the serious Glencoe routes are hikes, not quick family stops.
6. Glen Etive — 60–80 min driving + photo stops | HIGH but FLEXIBLE
Walking: roughly 100–400 m total; mostly get out beside designated stopping areas. This is about the single-track scenic drive, Buachaille Etive Mòr views and short photo/video stops. If late: turn back earlier. You do not need to drive all the way to Loch Etive to have the experience.
7. 15 By The Shore — target 19:15–19:45 | CHECK-IN
Walking: negligible. Your booking allows check-in from 16:00; that does not mean you need to arrive at 16:00. All sightseeing above is planned before the hotel.
8. Fort William Indian dinner — ~20:00 onward
After check-in, the plan is dinner only — no more sightseeing. This keeps the evening easy after the long drive.

If you lose 30 minutes: shorten Stirling first. If you lose another 30 minutes: make Tyndrum fuel/toilet only and shorten Glen Etive. Do not cut: Rannoch Moor or Three Sisters/Glencoe.

Must-do: Rannoch Moor + Three Sisters + Glencoe. Best optional: Glen Etive. Skip if late: Stirling Castle interior first, then Glen Etive if weather turns poor.

🥾 Extra hikes for us — while parents rest

Important: the Three Sisters viewpoint itself is not a 45-minute “Three Sisters Trail”. The famous walk starting there is Lost Valley / Coire Gabhail, a real mountain walk: about 4 km return, 2–3 hours, ~335 m ascent, with steep rocky ground, mild scrambling and a river crossing. It is excellent, but it is not a quick add-on.

Stirling — no extra hike today
The castle hill already adds uphill/cobbled walking. Save your legs and, more importantly, save the clock for the Highlands.
Rannoch Moor / Loch Tulla — 10–20 min wandering only
There is no need to chase a trail here. Use the safe viewpoints for short walks and photography. Worth it: YES for scenery. Worth spending 45 min hiking: NO on this itinerary.
Three Sisters → Lost Valley — ⭐ best serious hike, but 2–3 hours
Full route: ~4 km return • 2–3 hr • ~335 m ascent • rough/steep with some scrambling. This is the genuinely special hiking option between the Three Sisters. Today: only attempt the full route if you consciously drop/shorten later stops and conditions are good. Do not treat the first 20 minutes as a guaranteed mini-version of the Lost Valley: the actual hidden valley is the reward and you will not reach it in a 45-minute return window.
An Torr / Signal Rock — ⭐ best easy 45-minute option
Blue trail: ~1¼ miles • ~45 min • easy woodland trail to Signal Rock. Yellow: ~¾ mile • ~25 min. Black: just over 1 mile • ~1 hr. This is the cleanest choice if we want a real named walk without destroying the road-trip schedule. Parents can rest at/near the parking area while we do Blue, or everyone can choose the shorter Yellow trail.
Glencoe Lochan — best 45-minute “work the legs” option
Lochan Trail: 1.6 km • ~30 min • easy, mostly flat. Woodland Trail: 1.6 km • ~45 min • strenuous grade, rocky/rooty and undulating. Mountain Trail: 1.6 km • ~45 min • strenuous grade with loose gravel, uneven steps and long steep gradients, but better elevated views. Best split: parents do/rest by the Lochan while fitter walkers take the Mountain Trail. This requires a small detour through Glencoe village, so choose it instead of An Torr, not as well as An Torr.
Loch Achtriochtan — 10–20 min only
Easy short walk from the parking area toward the loch for reflections and mountain views. Worth it: YES if the view is clear. No need to turn it into a long hike.
Glen Etive — flexible 10–30 min walks from the car
The road itself is the experience. Stop only in safe/appropriate places and walk short sections beside the glen/river. A published road walk is variable and can be 1–3 hours, but there is no reason to do the whole thing today. Worth it: short exploratory walks YES; a long hike NO.

My hiking choice today: if conditions are decent and we have 45 minutes, do An Torr Blue / Signal Rock. If we specifically want a steeper workout and mountain views, do the Glencoe Lochan Mountain Trail. Save the full Lost Valley for a day with 2–3 hours and suitable weather.

Weather rule: if the ground is very wet, rain is heavy or streams are high, stay with the short waymarked woodland/lochan options. Do not improvise higher mountain routes. Today’s forecast is showery and windy, so reassess when you physically reach Glencoe.

Parents: only leave them in the car if you are legally/safely parked and they are comfortable waiting. At a formal trail car park, a café/visitor area or an easy lochside option is usually nicer than waiting in a roadside lay-by.

👟 Walking plan — parent-friendly

Overall: very parent-friendly because most of the day is scenic driving. Expect only about 1–2 km total walking if you use the easy version.

Stirling: make the castle interior optional. Parents can do the exterior/viewpoint and café while anyone who wants more walking goes inside.

Rannoch Moor / Three Sisters / Glencoe: these are mainly roadside viewpoints. Most stops can be enjoyed with 5–15 minutes of walking from the car.

Glen Etive: ideal for the parents — stay with the car and use short photo stops rather than walking a trail.

Flexible split: no one needs to miss the main scenery. If energy is low, remove Stirling Castle interior and keep Glencoe + Glen Etive.

Best rest points: Callander lunch, Tyndrum coffee/fuel, Glencoe visitor stops. Skip first if tired: Stirling interior.

Weather plan
