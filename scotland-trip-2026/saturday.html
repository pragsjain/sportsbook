👟 Walking plan — parent-friendly

Overall: very parent-friendly because most of the day is scenic driving. Expect only about 1–2 km total walking if you use the easy version.

Stirling: make the castle interior optional. Parents can do the exterior/viewpoint and café while anyone who wants more walking goes inside.

Rannoch Moor / Three Sisters / Glencoe: these are mainly roadside viewpoints. Most stops can be enjoyed with 5–15 minutes of walking from the car.

Glen Etive: ideal for the parents — stay with the car and use short photo stops rather than walking a trail.

Flexible split: no one needs to miss the main scenery. If energy is low, remove Stirling Castle interior and keep Glencoe + Glen Etive.

Best rest points: Callander lunch, Tyndrum coffee/fuel, Glencoe visitor stops. Skip first if tired: Stirling interior.

Weather plan

Western Highlands are typically wetter and windier than Edinburgh. Expect cool 12–15°C conditions, showers and rapid changes. Waterproof jacket and good shoes are essential.
